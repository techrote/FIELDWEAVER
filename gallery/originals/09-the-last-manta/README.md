## Actual image inspection — admission deliberately unresolved

On 11 November 2026 the committed canonical PNG was reopened at its native 480 × 320 size and compared with the committed actual CPU PNGs of original 08 *Registry of Vacant Rooms*, original 07 *Zero Meridian*, and original 04 *Pollen Observatory*. The accepted Operator Atlas / Timeline Pulse forms were also visible in the previously archived Firefox canonical-gallery screenshot; that is a **UI screenshot of accepted CPU proofs**, not an independent new full-size export.

- **Form and silhouette:** two long, nearly symmetrical pointed wings make a recognizable glider-like sign, with thin internal ribs and a tiny centre. Unlike original 08's three segregated vertical rectangular frames, original 07's interrupted concentric compass arcs, or original 04's five separate curled seedheads, the principal Manta form spans almost the entire horizontal axis.
- **Negative space and density:** the interior of both fins is intentionally empty. Only 2.88% of output pixels differ from the ground; the competing Rooms has 8,452/131,328 (~6.44%) non-background pixels and Meridian 3,041/101,376 (~3.00%) in different crops. Percentages convey sparse occupancy, **not** an aesthetic metric or pixel-for-pixel similarity test.
- **Palette and mark:** an exceptionally fine cyan outline and pale vertical/diagonal ribs dominate; the lower violet segmented wake is visually weaker. Rooms' ochre / chalk / violet frame separation and Pollen's saturated terracotta and coral whorls show substantially stronger colour-area differentiation. The Manta is visibly distinct in macro-silhouette but less resolved in body/anatomical detail.
- **Comparative verdict:** **hold for independent artistic decision, not automatic admission**. Its outstanding strength is the paired wing contour; its liability is a little too much unstructured black and a centre that reads more like a drawn joint than a living creature. Increasing random detail or treating unique hashes as proof of quality would not address that issue. The file is not an exact duplicate of the inspected compositions, but semantic visual similarity with *all* outstanding originals has not been exhaustively established.

The exhibition comparison above is the single most useful Integration action: view this piece alongside the accepted forms and Rooms in Firefox, then either accept the spare glider-like direction or request a **specific** body/wake revision.
