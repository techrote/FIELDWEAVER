Provisional curatorial decision

Review 09 first for separated gestures. Keep 06 for its cyan basin, and 01 where a denser colour counterpoint is wanted. Hold 08 beside 09 rather than admitting both reflexively; they share related cyan/violet interruption patterns. Deprioritize 05 for a space-constrained first exhibition. This is a recommendation, not owner aesthetic acceptance.
