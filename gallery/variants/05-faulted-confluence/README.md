# Faulted Confluence — the suspended carrier

Two generations of topology-safe FW-011 mutation applied to the immutable accepted Operator Atlas preset. The first generation performs a six-operator bold compound mutation, then a second scoped mutation. Twenty-four independent and replayable branches explore how stacked field and emitter disruptions can reorganize a familiar current.

## Selected composition

Sibling 16 (contact-sheet panel 2) was chosen after actual inspection of the FW-012 CPU canonical contact sheet and the exact selected PNG, not merely its morphology rank. The original down-slanting cyan arc becomes a suspended, nearly horizontal cyan two-bend ribbon with a light inner seam. A small ochre particulate cluster above, a clipped violet strip high left, and a single vivid red-green registration mark below supply restrained counterweights. Much of the plate remains black by intention.

The other finalists retain a tall cyan arch (panel 3), a downturned hook (panel 4), a disconnected set of fragments (panel 5), or a diagonal rainbow exit (panel 6). Unlike the previously submitted Countercurrent Canticle and Negative Loom, this piece has no opposing rising arc; its empty lower-right field is part of its formal tension.
